## Side Quest Split
ZCOM had 12 side missions, 2 each for the 6 Operator NPCs. Aside from the 6 NPCs you could take on missions, you had Bennic, Runa, M-3VO, and Neesh, adding another 4 NPCs as shopkeeps or utility
# Resistance
1) Amadeus
Amadeus's quest focuses on his incredible invention in the hand-cannon, as well as how he differs in revolution against Nomad. nomad should obv be saying that wolfmother has consolidated power too closely and is being too volatile and vain, letting her character flaws lash out on a national scale due to the power she wields. this cannot be allowed to persist, and as such the resistance needs to become the new power base to better help the common man who is currently facing oppression at the hands of a tyrant. however, it needs to be pointed out that this is probably what Leila said about the kings before them, and he needs to be careful as to not become his mothers' son.
Amadeus' weapon should also be a key target for the wolfpack, as they'd want to replicate it for mass production, but this should be established earlier as it comes up in the main plot during the coliseum raid. Plagueknight infiltrating the coliseum should be a slow out-of-mission thing, where you see her disguise as a civilian just walking around the coliseum (though the player doesn't know) and its a slow buildup. Completing amadeus' quest shows you his romance with her, and adds to the coliseum raid by giving special cutscenes where amadeus gets blasted
2) Plague Doctor
Plague doctor's quests deal in the other way that Nomad can be clashed against, in that he doesn't care about who actually wins the war for power so long as his izinor campaign is funded. Plague doctor opposes nomad on a moral level, less an ideological or political one, and the missions should be things dealing with that difference and the clash of plague doctor killing or advocating for such
The other parts of plague doctor's questline would be his warning of the conflict in izinor, pertaining more to his origin. We don't see any actual izinorian threat, but we do get a more in depth character study of where plague doctor came from and why he acts the way he does
plague doctor vs nomad should be "should we resort to unethical tactics to win" and not "do unethical tactics work at all". a point to be made it that plague doctor is right and that his murder of the innocent and guilty alike force the wolfpack down. it should be the dilemma that its wrong but its easier and more effective, but still most definitely wrong
for the theme of plague doctor's "war" in izinor, there should be multiple opportunities to invest intel and whatnot to the border, but you'll notice that these are kinda expensive and don't have any benefit to the wolfpack front, only really buffing plague doctor bond, which helps put the player in nomad's shoes of the dilemma of what plague doctor's asking
3) Khada
Khada could be about the magical side as well as some background on her and Leila, etc before elwin's death, giving some prequel lore about the origins of a lot of the villains and the stuff surrounding that

## Nomad's Den (the Coliseum)
# Map (Holotable)
Similar split between influence, operations, and missions
Operations and Missions are identical to ZCOM, except that sending operators on operations (the non-combat stuff) takes multiple cycles to complete and they'll be unusable during that time for missions or level ups, etc, incentivizing the player to have a wide roster of resistance members (wide is like more than 8 lmao)
For influence, I think the bonuses and contacts and other things like that is fine, and we'd keep like 90% of the stuff ZCOM does, but I'd like to do the XCOM thing where you don't start with every region and have to build up to them by establishing contact and stuff using your contact resource.
Some regions should only unlock after certain campaign missions as well
Dunno about you but I think because this is a medieval revolution and its more of a war, the cycles should represent weeks not days
Here, along with other relevant UI menus like recruitment and black market, will also display the global reputation of the resistance
Because of polynex's theme of "progress and adaptation wins" we'd have the Wolfpack activities you sabotage and they upgrade themselves. However, because polynex is a game with themes, I think the status quo faction (regular kingdoms + black legion) should be stronger by default but never evolve
Leveling Up Units
I think we should use the XCOM system where instead of a focus tree we have a rank up system, allowing for streamlined leveling since you'll have to do it for a wide number of operators. It's not much different from ZCOM, just making more sense for the resistance side of things. We'll still keep bonds like in ZCOM providing unit-specific stat upgrades and abilities like teamwork from xcom or backup from zcom.
Because we also have the upgrade table, we could shift to have some of the "operator hp +4" buffs to the upgrade table to be global, and keep the bond stuff to only be big milestones with more tangible upgrades like in XCOM (or the final bond upgrades in ZCOM)

# Reputation
Reputation should affect quality and quantity of the black market, and the quality and quantity of operators you can recruit (a super low rep would only show you like 3 recruits, and they're all starting out lower rank. Also refreshing recruits should cost intel idk why zcom made it free it usually isn't). The side missions and dilemmas n stuff should raise and lower the rep score in addition to whatever else they do
# Recruiting
certain areas on the map should unlock new types of recruits (either when you complete a certain level of influence in that area / perform a mission). Access to the coliseum should grant you gladiator recruits, Astoria should be crusaders / kingdom crashers, etc.
A higher reputation increases the number of slots in the recruitment menu, and the further down slots have better chances for higher rank recruits
# Black Market
No reason not to do it, i think utilities or weapons to sub out make sense for the combat
A higher reputation increases the number of slots in the black market, and the further down slots have better chances for higher quality items
# Expansion
Aside from the initial contact operations (XCOM has a single "reach out" operation) and upgrading influence in a region to get rewards and buffs, we could do the opposite thing where when a territory gets max influence you get a new mission on the holotable to finalize the liberation, such as taking out a wolfpack stronghold. it allows more encounters with wolfpack leaders and can provide more tangible rewards after the mission completes, and feels more like a proper accomplishment. Completing this mission would impose a decent debuff on the wolfpack kingdom, and while they can still attack the region it won't be as powerful as before
## Ajax's Den (the Wolfpack Den)
# Fighting the Resistance
It would be really interesting if as you level up the resistance for nomad's operators, you level up the resistance enemies Ajax faces (getting the influence all operators have +4 hp also gives 4hp to Ajax's enemies). It adds an interesting layer for the player, as it's a trade off of making half the game easier and the other half sometimes harder, and could help reflect the ideology the player ends up agreeing with more
Because you're recruiting jobbers to the resistance, the randomly generated bozos, it would be cool if we punctuated a couple of Ajax's missions against the resistance with a resistance operator who's in your way. As you fight them, the spare/capture/execute becomes a real choice, as spare lets them return home with injuries, capture takes the operator away and means nomad needs to launch a new rescue mission to get them back, and execute fucking slimes him out permanently. You could technically not attack the operator very much at all and they'd return home with no injuries, but you can imagine how Ajax's life is harder during that mission, so it depends on how much you like that operator or whatever

# Leveling Up Units
Instead of rank, we would instead do a more focus tree type system where completing missions and upgrading bonds gives you skill points to put into the branches
Recruiting
Very similar to nomad's den, you can recruit jobbers to the wolfpack. We may differentiate some of the character traits

# Utilities
Ajax's den would ditch the black market, recruitment, and influence systems, so he'd get all his non-skill tree stuff from other NPCs around the den, like Virgil or Vindus
In the wander section of missions we should put some harder-to-find tarot cards (3 per) which serve as a reading of the mission and serve as collectibles. Virgil can be the shopkeeper to take these as currency or something
# Expansion
To contrast the resistance choosing how and where to invest to expand, wolfmother basically barges into ajax's room and goes "its time" to start a kingdom capture mission. The wolfpack expanding to a new territory will shift from status quo faction elements to more primarily wolfpack, which can increase the difficulty of the game since the wolfpack gets stronger but the status quo stays the same

## Combat Novelties (things I'd feel the need to specify)
# Surrender State
Enemies falling below X% hp puts them in a surrender state. Your options are Spare (they run away), Capture (probably requires an operator to use some AP to tie them up but grants intel), and Execute (maybe gives some combat advantage like restoring AP, and the extra damage as a free hit makes combat easier, maybe demoralizes surrounding enemies). 
Spare should raise reputation, execute should lower it. Defeating an enemy outside of this shouldn't affect rep
Aside from dropping hp, some enemies can hit a demoralized score high enough that they'll surrender as well. Stronger enemies surrendering will give some demoralized to weaker allies based on their rank, so you can cause a chain reaction
Some enemies simply would never surrender and would rather die, we'll give that a passive
# Armor (ZCOM Defense)
Your units and enemies defense should have the XCOM display of a series of hexagons (for us shields prob) indicating the defense value. I just prefer the UI they used, since ZCOM had some mystical value you had to examine them for. We should also be more obvious about armor shred / armor pierce abilities

We could do the thing where magic goes through physical armor, and physical goes through magical armor, and certain abilities shred certain armor types
Unknown if we want to have an unshreddable armor type
# Dismembered
Adding custom models and anims for when units lose certain limbs would be a hassle, its fine. We can do the swgoh disarmed thing where it reduces their combat capabilities but there isn't a crazy visual for it
Dismembered should be inflicted by certain strong abilities, and should reduce chance-to-hit and movement i think. I also think it should prob prevent Advantage abilities
Plot relevant characters and also important or strong enemies gain "Structurally Sound" passive which prevents execution and dismemberment. The exception is Ajax
